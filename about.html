Our Core Mission

Bridging Ground Sensors with Global Logistics

Over 30% of global agricultural harvests are lost during transport due to unmonitored microclimate shifts, cold-chain reefer failures, and poor visibility.

At Stackly Agri, our mission is to empower growers and distributors with continuous, tamper-proof IoT telemetry data—ensuring every harvest reaches table markets at peak quality.

Real-Time Soil & Canopy Intelligence

Multi-depth moisture & NPK probes streaming data every 10 seconds.

Zero-Break Cold Chain Tracking

Active GPS & thermal sensors inside refrigerated transport trucks.

Our Technology Stack

Built on Three Uncompromising Agritech Pillars

Discover how hardware telemetry, satellite diagnostics, and blockchain certificates work seamlessly together.

1. Ground IoT Telemetry

Low-power wireless sensors log soil pH, moisture matrix potential, and ambient frost warnings directly to the cloud dashboard.

2. Active Reefer Fleet GPS

Continuous thermal logging inside cargo containers ensures cargo stays within exact temperature thresholds during transit.

3. Spectral Drone & Satellite

High-resolution NDVI canopy mapping detects crop stress, water deficit, and pest hotspots before visual symptoms show.
